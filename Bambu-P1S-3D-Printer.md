Description
-----------

This is an FDM printer. It takes 1.75mm filament, PLA, ABS or PET(G) are available to use. This printer can use multiple colours in a single print - up to 4 colours at once. 

[See Bambu store for more details](https://uk.store.bambulab.com/products/p1s)

The slicer for this printer is [Bambu Studio](https://bambulab.com/en/download/studio). This is installed on the laptop in the Fab room, and ready to use. Bambu studio slices STL files into GCODE files, and also communicates with the printer.
